Three directions for Plantry

Each direction shows the same planned week (Sat 31 Oct to Fri 6 Nov) on the desktop week board, plus the phone Today screen for Monday. Same layout and controls in all three; only type, colour, density and one signature detail change. That signature detail is how a batch and its leftovers are marked.

Chosen · Cookbook with batch badges

Direction B's serif headings, quiet paper ground and ink-blue actions. Batches use A's badges directly on the slots: "Cook × 4" where it is cooked, "2/4" on each leftover, in the batch's colour.

A · Market

Produce colours from the market stall: beet, squash, leek. A deep leek-green shell. Signature: every batch is a coloured price tag; its leftover plates carry the same tag with the portion count.

B · Cookbook

A well-used cookbook: serif headings, quiet paper ground, ink-blue actions. Signature: a batch is a coloured margin rule with an italic note ("Chili, day 2 of 4"), like a handwritten annotation.

C · Prep tape

The meal-prep kitchen: stainless greys, a graphite shell, condensed labels. Signature: leftovers wear masking-tape labels like real prep containers ("Chili 2/4, use by Wed").

Example data only. Batches: Chili sin carne × 4 (cooked Sunday), Thai green curry × 3 (cooked Wednesday), Pasta al limone × 1 (Saturday). Tuesday dinner and Friday dinner are marked "No plate".
